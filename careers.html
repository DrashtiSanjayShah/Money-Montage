CAREERS

Help people build a strong future

Whether you are a fresher with a vision to help people achieve their financial ambitions, or a corporate professional looking to take a leap towards an entrepreneurial practise in the area of personal finance – we will be happy to talk to you to see how you could be part of the team at Money Montage.

What you need

  • CFP CM (Certified Financial Planner) certification, or commitment to upskill
  • Experience in sectors like banking, investment management, mutual funds
  • Commitment towards working long-term with clients
  • Openness towards reskilling with required certifications

As a Money Montage team member, you get to:

  • Meet people from all walks of life
  • Understand their goals for the future
  • Gain satisfaction from helping people achieve their dreams or achieve their goals
  • Get the support of a team of planners from diverse backgrounds

Irrespective of your current background, if you find yourself keen to explore the field of personal finances, and wish to make your work meaningful by helping people live a financial confident life, we would love to hear from you,
