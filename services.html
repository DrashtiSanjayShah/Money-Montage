Our services

Lead a financially confident life

Building a sound future for yourself and loved ones is possible once you prioritise your financial goals and follow a systematic, well-charted path towards it. Be it providing the best education to your child, living a comfortable independent retired life, or saving up an emergency healthcare fund – We are here to help you with the most appropriate solutions to build your dream future.

Values at Money Montage

Personalised Path -

Gain clarity about the financial way forward with a Suitability Report that’s custom-created to match your goals, time frame, and present-day financial life.

Objective Eye +

Be confident of every decision ahead as we offer you our unbiased suggestions, always keeping your goals in mind.

Accountability +

Stay assured with regular reviews of your progress, and suggestions to ensure your investments stay aligned to your financial goals.

Lasting Partnership +

Count on us to partner you on this long-term financial journey, with our experience, meticulous research, and objective recommendations.

01

Our first talk

You are welcome to a no-obligations introductory meeting with a Relationship Manager (RM), where we discuss at length about your life, needs, ambitions, interests, and the dream future you wish to create. Your financial goals are at the heart of what we do, and your customised service starts with knowing you better.

02

Receive a personalised suitability report

Based on your financial goals, current lifestyle, and time frame, your RM charts a suitability report that takes into account an array of appropriate options like mutual funds, life insurance, medical insurance, and portfolio management services. We set this as an organised path towards achieving your ambitions.

03

Start your financial journey

Once you are happy with the proposal, our operations team takes over the responsibility and carries out the transactions approved by you. You have begun the journey towards steadily reaching your financial goals.

04

Receive regular reviews

Keeping an eye on your investments is important. Your RM will present a review report once every six months, to help keep track of progress, as well as make any suitable adjustments that support your financial goals better.

05

Best-in-class Tech

Our services are powered by Salesforce – the world’s best CRM platform, that ensures you have a seamless experience and well-documented review sessions. We use Ace-MF and Morningstar - the best mutual fund analysis tools in the market, which help us hold richer conversations about the most appropriate products for your objectives.
