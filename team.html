Meet the Team

Prioritising your financial future

We are dedicated and certified experts with diverse backgrounds and a single purpose – helping you chart the best route towards achieving your financial goals.

01

Certified expertise

Each Relationship Manager (RM) holds the CFP CM (Certified Financial Planner) certification, to be able to offer you clarity and objective recommendations throughout your journey towards your financial goals. They also hold the NISM-VA certification as required by AMFI regulations for mutual fund distributors.

02

Diverse and multi-disciplinary

Our RMs come from diverse backgrounds of being equity market specialists, engineers, MBAs, masters in nuclear physics, equity traders, to list a few. We learn from each other’s varied perspective, and use our collective knowledge to help you better.

03

Continuous learning

The team continues to upgrade itself through additional certifications, seminars, research and reviews with fund managers. We also host monthly masterclass sessions with external industry specialists and in-house experts, to constantly strengthen our capabilities for you.
